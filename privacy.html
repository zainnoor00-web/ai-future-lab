Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

AI Future Lab Worker is a tool used by the AI Future Lab channel operator to upload original English and Urdu video content to YouTube.

Information accessed

When authorized by the operator, the tool uses Google OAuth credentials and the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set associated metadata. It may process the operator's channel authorization, generated videos, titles, descriptions, and upload identifiers. The tool is not offered for general public sign-in.

How information is used

Access is used only for operating the AI Future Lab publishing workflow. OAuth credentials and production files are stored in the operator-controlled environment and are not sold or used for advertising.

Sharing and retention

Videos and related metadata are sent to YouTube when the operator initiates or schedules publishing. Local authentication tokens and workflow records are retained as needed to operate the workflow and may be deleted by the operator. Google's handling of data is governed by its own policies.

Access and revocation

The operator can revoke the app's Google account access through Google Account permissions. Questions and deletion requests can be sent to zainnoor00@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the workflow or data practices change.
